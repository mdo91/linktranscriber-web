## Insight Studio workflow and visible evidence

First-party Swift interface source reviewed on 20 November verifies this flow for a **completed, nonempty transcript**:

1. Open the transcript from **Library**, then open the wand-and-stars **Insight Studio** control. A wider toolbar shows its name; a compact toolbar can show the icon with the same accessibility label.
2. Output choices are grouped under **Understand**, **Apply** and **Study**. In Study, choose **Study guide**, **Quiz** or **Flashcards** as appropriate.
3. Choose **Output language**. **Customize** is optional. For Study guide, the verified fields include **Topic focus (optional)** and **Learning or exam objective (optional)**. **Tailor to me** is optional and requires a goal when enabled.
4. Review the displayed Insight cost, then choose **Generate**. The saved result opens in **Outputs**, while **Transcript** remains available.
5. Read the result and its **Source excerpt** alongside the recording. Use **Edit** where the selected output offers it. Excerpts help find supporting material; they do not establish automatic factual verification.

These labels come from first-party interface source. Do not infer identical toolbar placement on Mac or every device from iPad captures. The released feature evidence and the staging screenshot evidence have different scopes.

## Processing and purchases

Sign in with Apple is required for online processing and purchases. Processing consumes media minutes and Insight credits. Users can check both balances, usage history and processing cost before confirming. Available plans and optional top-up packs are shown in the app. Do not claim that every online task requires a Premium subscription, invent prices or quotas, or promise unlimited free processing. Internet and consent for AI processing remain required. Only process material the user is authorized to use.

Private, restricted and live videos are unsupported; public sources can also be inaccessible. Availability, processing time and accuracy vary. Do not promise media downloads, file uploads, offline processing, dubbing, embedded subtitles or unsupported import/export formats. A translation is readable text, not dubbed audio or embedded subtitles.
