When two people speak at once, use a Damas transcript to find the passage, then replay the original recording to label each turn you can identify. Keep an unknown speaker label on clear words you cannot assign and mark any words you cannot hear as unclear. Add these labels yourself in a working copy.

## Create a transcript and find two clear turns

Once the transcript is available, copy the relevant passage into a working note. Keep the original link and record the start and end of the overlap using the source player. Replay from a little before the overlap to a little after it. Look for a clear turn from each voice in that stretch, ideally when the recording shows the person speaking or when the moderator addresses them by name and they answer. If you can tell the voices apart but cannot identify the people, keep neutral labels such as Speaker A and Speaker B.

## Mark the turn and the confidence in each part

- **Clear speaker and clear words.** Put those words on their own line with the confirmed speaker label.
- **Clear words, uncertain speaker.** Use `[speaker uncertain]` before the words. Keep the uncertainty on that line even if neighboring lines are clear.
- **Clear speaker, uncertain words.** Keep the speaker label and put `[unclear]` at the point where the words become indistinct.
- **Two voices audible together.** Use separate lines for what you can hear from each person. Mark the shared span `[overlap]`. Resume ordinary labels once the voices separate.

Then replay the span straight through and read the lines in order. Check that you preserved the interruption and that the resumed turn still belongs to the right person. If a visible speaker reacts while someone else is talking, use the reaction as a cue to replay the passage. A reaction alone cannot identify the voice.

## Worked example with a moderator and two guests

In this fictional panel, a guest starts to describe a timetable and another voice interrupts. The camera cuts to the audience during part of the exchange. These times come from the example’s source player.

| Time | Working transcript | What the replay supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:12 | Guest A: The pilot starts in May— | Guest A is visibly speaking before the interruption. |
| 08:14–08:16 | [overlap] [speaker uncertain]: In two schools. | The interjection is audible over Guest A. The camera cuts to the audience, and the second voice is hard to place. |
| 08:14–08:16 | [overlap] Guest A: —in the north district. | Guest A’s voice continues under the interjection. The camera returns in time to show Guest A visibly saying the last words. |
| 08:18 | Moderator: Let’s move to the budget. | The moderator is visibly speaking after the overlap. |

Keep the two-school detail under the uncertain label. The notes can still record Guest A’s timetable and the moderator’s transition. If the finished piece needs somebody to stand behind the two-school detail, return to the recording or hold that detail until you can verify its attribution.

## Check the labels before sharing

Take one final pass over the uncertain lines. Keep each time reference tied to the original recording and carry any unresolved label forward when you copy the passage into another document. If you are preparing a direct quotation, use the [interview quote guide](/guides/verify-interview-quotes/) to confirm exact wording and context. If you are comparing the guests’ positions, carry only confirmed attributions into your [interview theme summary](/guides/interview-theme-summary/).
